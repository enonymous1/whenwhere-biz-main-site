Weddings & Private Events

Production that disappears — so the party doesn’t have to

Central Virginia’s best venues weren’t built for modern production. We’ve run sound at them anyway — load-in routes, power quirks, and noise ordinances handled before your guests arrive. No hand-offs, no gaps, no awkward silences.

Wedding DJ & MC flow

Custom-curated music that sounds like you, pacing that keeps the night moving, and an MC who guides the room without ever stealing it. We build your run-of-show with your planner and caterer and cue every transition.

Vineyard & remote audio

Vows at the top of the vineyard, cocktails on the terrace, dinner in the barn. Multi-zone systems put clear sound exactly where your guests are — even where there’s no power.

Ambiance & lighting

Light is the fastest way to make a barn feel like a ballroom. Wireless uplighting and dance-floor washes shift the room from golden hour to late night as the energy builds.

Questions

Before you ask

What exactly does WhenWhere do?

Two things, one team: DJ & MC and full event production for weddings and private events, and drone light shows, launching with a 2027 founding season. Book one, or stack them — a wedding with a drone finale is our favourite kind of night.

Where do you work?

Charlottesville and Central Virginia are home — Albemarle County, the Blue Ridge foothills, and the vineyard and estate country around them. Further afield in Virginia? Ask in the inquiry and we’ll talk.

How far ahead should we book?

As early as you can — fall Saturdays and UVA alumni weekends go first, and drone shows need extra lead time for design and airspace authorisations. Even a rough date in the inquiry below holds your place in the conversation.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the event — venue, guest count, production, and how custom you want it — so everything is quoted individually rather than off a one-size price list. Send the inquiry with a rough budget and we’ll tell you honestly what it can do.

We’re a venue or a planner — can we partner?

Yes — venues and planners are some of our favourite people. Choose “Venue or planner — partnership” in the form below and tell us about your property or your clients.

About

About Us

Finding inspiration in every turn. WhenWhere is an event production company based in Charlottesville, serving weddings and private events across Central Virginia — the DJ & MC, the sound, the lighting, and, from 2027, the sky itself.

Our Story

WhenWhere started with a simple observation: the nights people remember aren’t the ones with the fanciest kit — they’re the ones where everything just worked. The vows landed clearly at the back row. The speeches were crisp. Dinner flowed into dancing without a gap, and the floor never emptied.

So we built one team to own all of it — DJ & MC flow, multi-zone audio for vineyards and remote sites, and lighting that turns a barn into a ballroom — under a single contract and a single point of contact, with backup gear for everything that matters. No hand-offs, no gaps, no awkward silences.

In 2027 we add our newest chapter: drone light shows, writing couples’ initials, dates, and logos in light above their guests. Same crew, same obsession — a bigger canvas.
